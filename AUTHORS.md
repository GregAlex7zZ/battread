# Authorship and AI contribution

**Author and maintainer: Alessandro Gregucci.**

Alessandro initiated the project, defined its requirements,
directed its implementation, and made scientific and product decisions,
including Bio-Logic dq precedence and Neware CSV Total Time selection.

OpenAI Codex was used extensively throughout development. Its contribution
included reviewing specifications, proposing implementation decisions,
generating and editing a substantial part of the library code, writing tests
and documentation, creating demonstration notebooks, and running automated
validation, benchmarks and package checks. AI assistance was part of the
implementation process, rather than limited to occasional suggestions.

The test and acceptance reports describe the automated checks and fixture
comparisons actually performed. They do not establish an independent human
review of every generated contribution. Scientific review, maintenance and
release approval remain human responsibilities.

Third-party libraries and fixtures retain their original attribution and
licenses; see THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md. This statement records contributions
and does not replace those notices or assign copyright ownership.
